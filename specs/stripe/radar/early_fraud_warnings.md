# Early Fraud Warning

An early fraud warning indicates that the card issuer has notified us that a charge may be fraudulent.

Related guide: [Early fraud warnings](https://docs.stripe.com/docs/disputes/measuring.md#early-fraud-warnings)

## Endpoints

### Retrieve an early fraud warning

- [GET /v1/radar/early_fraud_warnings/:id](early_fraud_warnings/retrieve.md)

### List all early fraud warnings

- [GET /v1/radar/early_fraud_warnings](early_fraud_warnings/list.md)
