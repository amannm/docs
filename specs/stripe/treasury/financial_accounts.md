# Financial Accounts

Stripe Treasury provides users with a container for money called a FinancialAccount that is separate from their Payments balance. FinancialAccounts serve as the source and destination of Treasury’s money movement APIs.

## Endpoints

### Create a FinancialAccount

- [POST /v1/treasury/financial_accounts](financial_accounts/create.md)

### Update a FinancialAccount

- [POST /v1/treasury/financial_accounts/:id](financial_accounts/update.md)

### Retrieve a FinancialAccount

- [GET /v1/treasury/financial_accounts/:id](financial_accounts/retrieve.md)

### List all FinancialAccounts

- [GET /v1/treasury/financial_accounts](financial_accounts/list.md)
