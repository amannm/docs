# Credit Reversals

You can reverse some [ReceivedCredits](credit_reversals.md#received_credits) depending on their network and source flow. Reversing a ReceivedCredit leads to the creation of a new object known as a CreditReversal.

## Endpoints

### Create a CreditReversal

- [POST /v1/treasury/credit_reversals](credit_reversals/create.md)

### Retrieve a CreditReversal

- [GET /v1/treasury/credit_reversals/:id](credit_reversals/retrieve.md)

### List all CreditReversals

- [GET /v1/treasury/credit_reversals](credit_reversals/list.md)
